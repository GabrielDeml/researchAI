## Metrics
- Ledger coverage: number of unique attempted reference seeds in attempted_reference_ledger.csv divided by 48; it must equal 1.0, with non-grokking and rule-ineligible seeds retained.
- Rule-specific restricted mean grokking time: for arm a, rule r, and split s, RMST_a(r,s) is the mean of min(observed grok time,5000) over reference-eligible paired seeds.
- Relative intervention reduction: E(r,s) = [RMST_ref(r,s)-RMST_double(r,s)]/RMST_ref(r,s), with positive values indicating faster grokking under the doubled learning rate.
- Post-hoc selection uplift: U = E(r_star,selection)/E(B,selection)-1; the first hypothesis threshold is U >= 0.30.
- Selection and held-out excess: D_selection = E(r_star,selection)-E(B,selection) and D_holdout = E(r_star,heldout)-E(B,heldout).
- Excess-retention fraction: R = D_holdout/D_selection when D_selection > 0; at least half the excess disappears when R <= 0.50.
- Eligibility and censoring counts: eligible pair count plus reference and doubled-rate non-grok counts for every rule and split, including a minimum of 6 pairs for the selected rule in each split.
- Primary binary decision: SUPPORTED only when E(B,selection)>0, U>=0.30, D_selection>0, R<=0.50, and all required sample-size conditions hold; otherwise REFUTED.
