# Topic

Topic Empirical AI/ML research. Pick one open, concrete question about the behavior of machine-learning models — for example: training dynamics and generalization (grokking, double descent), in-context learning in small transformers, calibration of model confidence, prompt/format sensitivity of language models, tokenization effects, or optimizer behavior — that can be investigated rigorously with small-scale experiments runnable on a single machine (CPU or Apple-Silicon MPS), and study it end-to-end.
