# Experiment design: Topic **Can the optimization comparison be repeated in a preregistered, informative regime?** Select the population size, mutation rate, and evaluation budget in advance using a separate calibration procedure so that serial success is measurable but nonsaturated. Then preregister the primary paired success effect, confidence interval, sample size, and decision rule before comparing batch-size-16. _(auto-enqueued follow-up from genetic-algorithms)_

**Hypothesis:** Across the same 40 deterministic calibration-validation splits, the median absolute deviation of serial success from the calibration target 0.50 will be at least 0.08 larger on held-out validation seeds than on the seeds used to select population size, mutation rate, and budget; reusing the selection seeds as confirmatory observations will therefore overstate calibration accuracy.

## Summary
Run a preregistered, deterministic steady-state genetic-algorithm experiment on 120 independent calibration-validation splits to quantify selection-induced optimism and then compare serial replacement with batch-size-16 replacement only on held-out seeds.

## Protocol
1. Use Python 3.11 or later with only numpy, pandas, and matplotlib; do not access the network or alter any specification after examining outcomes. Record package versions in the output.
2. Optimize the deterministic 20-bit OneMax objective f(x) = bit_count(x), whose unique success state is the all-ones bit string. Represent genomes as unsigned integers. A run is successful if the optimum is evaluated at least once within the evaluation budget.
3. Implement a generationally reproducible steady-state genetic algorithm. Initialize P genomes independently and uniformly from all 2^20 bit strings and count these P evaluations toward the budget. For every offspring evaluation, sample two population indices independently and uniformly with replacement; use the fitter genome as parent, breaking a fitness tie in favor of the first sampled index. Mutate each of the 20 bits independently with probability p. Evaluate the child. If its fitness is at least the current minimum population fitness, replace one minimum-fitness individual selected uniformly at random; otherwise discard it. Stop after exactly B total evaluations or immediately after evaluating the optimum. Use numpy.random.Generator(numpy.random.PCG64(seed)) separately for every run.
4. Preregister the candidate grid before running any calibration: population P in {4, 12}, mutation probability p in {0.5/20, 1/20, 2/20}, and total evaluation budget B in {80, 160, 320}, giving 18 settings. The target serial success rate is exactly 0.50.
5. Create 120 fixed, mutually disjoint calibration-validation splits indexed i = 0,...,119. For split i, set base_i = 10000000 + 10000*i. Its 16 calibration seeds are base_i + j for j = 0,...,15, and its 128 validation seeds are base_i + 1000 + j for j = 0,...,127. The same seed may be reused across candidate settings within its split as common random numbers, but no seed may occur in two splits or in both calibration and validation.
6. For every split and every candidate setting, run the serial algorithm on all 16 calibration seeds and compute its calibration success rate. Select the setting minimizing |calibration_success_rate - 0.50|. Resolve ties, in order, by smaller B, smaller P, and mutation-multiplier order 1, 0.5, 2. Save all candidate results and the selected setting; do not retune using validation results.
7. Apply each split's selected setting to the serial algorithm on that split's 128 held-out validation seeds. For split i compute c_i, the selected setting's success proportion on the 16 reused calibration seeds; v_i, its success proportion on the 128 held-out validation seeds; a_i = |c_i - 0.50|; b_i = |v_i - 0.50|; and delta_i = b_i - a_i.
8. Before inspecting validation outcomes, preregister the primary effect D = median(delta_i) over the 120 splits and the threshold 0.08. Construct a paired split-level 95% percentile bootstrap confidence interval by initializing PCG64 with seed 20250308, drawing 20000 bootstrap samples of 120 split indices with replacement, computing the median delta in each sample, and taking numpy.quantile values 0.025 and 0.975 with method='linear'. Support the hypothesis only if the lower confidence bound is at least 0.08; refute it only if the upper confidence bound is below 0.08; otherwise report inconclusive. Equality at 0.08 counts as support for a lower bound and does not count as refutation for an upper bound.
9. Treat informative calibration as a preregistered regime check: at least 96 of 120 selected calibration rates c_i must lie in [0.25, 0.75]. If this check fails, label the experiment 'regime-check failure/inconclusive' regardless of D; do not change the grid or rerun with new seeds.
10. Only after the settings and all rules above are locked, implement batch-size-16 replacement. Initialization, objective, tournament selection, mutation, replacement rule, seed, P, p, and B are identical to serial. At the start of each batch, freeze a snapshot of the population; generate up to 16 offspring, or the number remaining before budget B, with every tournament parent selected from that frozen snapshot. After all offspring in the batch have been evaluated, process them in generation order against the live population using the same serial replacement rule. Success means that the optimum was evaluated by any offspring, whether or not retained.
11. Run batch-size-16 on exactly the same 128 validation seeds and selected setting in each split; calibration seeds must not be used for this comparison. For each validation seed record paired binary outcomes for batch and serial. Define q_i as the mean within split i of batch_success - serial_success, and define the preregistered paired batch effect Q = mean(q_i) across the 120 splits. Obtain its 95% percentile cluster-bootstrap interval using the same 20000 split resamples used for D. Declare batch-size-16 superior if the lower bound is greater than 0, serial superior if the upper bound is less than 0, and otherwise declare no decisive difference. This batch decision is secondary and cannot change the hypothesis decision for D.
12. Also report median(a_i), median(b_i), D, the bootstrap interval for D, the count of splits with delta_i > 0, delta_i = 0, and delta_i < 0, the regime-check count, serial and batch validation success rates, Q, its interval, and paired discordant counts where only serial or only batch succeeds.
13. Save results.csv with one row per split and columns split, selected_P, selected_mutation_multiplier, selected_p, selected_B, calibration_success_rate, validation_serial_success_rate, validation_batch_success_rate, calibration_target_deviation, validation_target_deviation, delta, and batch_minus_serial. Save run_summary.json containing all aggregate metrics, confidence intervals, package versions, fixed seeds, decision labels, and runtime.
14. Save calibration_optimism.png at 1600 by 700 pixels with two panels. The left panel is a scatterplot of a_i versus b_i with a dashed y=x line and solid lines at the respective medians. The right panel plots the sorted delta_i values, a horizontal zero line, a horizontal 0.08 threshold line, and an annotation containing D and its 95% confidence interval. Use fixed plotting order and no random jitter.
15. Implement bit-packed genomes and avoid retaining per-evaluation histories. The fixed workload is 120 splits, 16 calibration seeds per split across 18 settings, and 128 validation seeds per split for each of serial and batch-size-16; do not add exploratory runs or increase budgets.

## Metrics
- Primary calibration-optimism effect D: median over 120 splits of |held-out serial success rate - 0.50| minus |reused calibration success rate - 0.50|.
- 95% paired bootstrap CI for D: 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles from 20000 fixed-seed split-level bootstrap resamples.
- Directional split count: numbers of splits with positive, zero, and negative delta_i, showing how consistently held-out deviation exceeds reused-calibration deviation.
- Informative-regime check: number of selected calibration success rates in [0.25, 0.75], required to be at least 96 of 120.
- Batch-size-16 paired success effect Q: mean over splits of the within-split mean of batch_success minus serial_success on the same 128 validation seeds.
- 95% cluster-bootstrap CI for Q and paired discordant outcome counts: seeds where only batch succeeds versus seeds where only serial succeeds.

## Time budget
30 minutes (ceiling 30).

## Expected outcomes
- **If supported:** The informative-regime check passes and the lower endpoint of the 95% paired bootstrap confidence interval for D is at least 0.08, with most splits having positive delta_i; this is classified as supported.
- **If refuted:** The informative-regime check passes and the upper endpoint of the 95% paired bootstrap confidence interval for D is below 0.08; this is classified as refuted. If the interval overlaps 0.08, the result is explicitly inconclusive.
