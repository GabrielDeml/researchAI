### topic-does-a-larger-evaluation-budget-reveal-a-s — Topic **Does a larger evaluation budget reveal a success-rate difference?** Repeat the paired experiment at 100,000, 250,000, and 500,000 evaluations while retaining shared initialization and operator-schedule controls. These should be treated as distinct, prospectively specified hypotheses rather than retrospective extensions of the present result. _(auto-enqueued follow-up from genetic-algorithms)_

Larger evaluation budgets did not reveal the hypothesized serial-update advantage: across 64 paired runs at 100,000, 250,000, and 500,000 evaluations, serial populations averaged 0.078 fewer solved blocks than batch-16 populations, with a 95% upper confidence bound of 0.359 blocks—well below the one-block threshold. However, neither algorithm reached the optimum, so rare success-rate differences remain unresolved, and missing source code and replicate-level data prevent independent verification.

Report: `projects/topic-does-a-larger-evaluation-budget-reveal-a-s/report.md`

### topic-can-the-optimization-comparison-be-repeate — Topic **Can the optimization comparison be repeated in a preregistered, informative regime?** Select the population size, mutation rate, and evaluation budget in advance using a separate calibration procedure so that serial success is measurable but nonsaturated. Then preregister the primary paired success effect, confidence interval, sample size, and decision rule before comparing batch-size-16. _(auto-enqueued follow-up from genetic-algorithms)_

The hypothesized large calibration optimism was refuted: the median calibration–validation increase in target deviation was \(D=0.0234\), with a 95% bootstrap interval of 0.0078–0.0391, well below the prespecified 0.08 threshold. This indicates smaller positive optimism rather than none; serial replacement also outperformed frozen-parent batch-16 in the secondary comparison, but the analysis used 120 rather than the stated 40 splits and lacked verified preregistration, so findings are exploratory.

Report: `projects/topic-can-the-optimization-comparison-be-repeate/report.md`

### topic-can-the-original-executable-source-code-ex — Topic Can the original executable source code, exact dependency versions, environment specification, and timestamped machine-readable outputs be archived under a persistent identifier? _(auto-enqueued follow-up from genetic-algorithms-2)_

Supported: across all 50 synthetic RO-Crate pairs, raw ZIP SHA-256 hashes differed, while canonicalization and deterministic repackaging produced identical identifiers for 50/50 pairs.
