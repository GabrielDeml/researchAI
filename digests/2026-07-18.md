### topic-can-the-original-executable-source-code-ex — Topic Can the original executable source code, exact dependency versions, environment specification, and timestamped machine-readable outputs be archived under a persistent identifier? _(auto-enqueued follow-up from genetic-algorithms-2)_

Supported for a restricted prototype: hashing raw ZIP bytes produced different identifiers for all 50 semantically equivalent pairs, while canonicalizing metadata and archive properties yielded identical identifiers for all 50. However, this demonstrates only implementation consistency on synthetic, policy-defined inputs—not general RO-Crate or JSON-LD semantic equivalence, cross-platform interoperability, or independently reproducible archival of executable source, dependencies, environments, and outputs.

Report: `projects/topic-can-the-original-executable-source-code-ex/report.md`

### topic-can-a-raw-per-seed-file-be-released-contai — Topic Can a raw per-seed file be released containing initialization attempts and misses, offspring attempts and misses, target counters, failure indicators, both scores, and per-run duplicate ratios for both algorithms? _(auto-enqueued follow-up from genetic-algorithms-2)_

Supported: algorithm-and-seed-keyed RNG streams produced byte-identical per-seed records for all 30 seeds when execution order was reversed, while sharing one mutable per-seed generator changed at least one audited field in every seed, including steady-state offspring attempts in 30/30 cases. A raw per-seed file can therefore expose these order effects, but the reported hashes and results remain independently unverified because the executable, artifacts, environment lockfile, and reproduction command were unavailable.

Report: `projects/topic-can-a-raw-per-seed-file-be-released-contai/report.md`

### genetic-algorithms-2 — Genetic algorithms

Refuted: the accounting-reversal criterion was not met. The \((\mu+1)\) GA produced 1.294 offspring attempts per cache-miss evaluation—below the 1.8 threshold—and its attempted-candidate median advantage remained greater than half its cache-miss advantage; only two of four required conditions held. Results apply to this implementation, configuration, 30 seeds, and accounting rules, with independent audit limited because code and raw records were unavailable.

Report: `projects/genetic-algorithms-2/report.md`

### genetic-algorithms — Genetic algorithms

The hypothesis was refuted: batch-16 replacement cut median end-to-end runtime by 87.0% (0.524 to 0.0680 seconds), but neither method reached the optimum in any of 30 runs, so the predicted ≥15-percentage-point success loss was not observed. This does not demonstrate equivalent optimization performance broadly, because the 50,000-evaluation budget produced a completely floored success rate and the comparison also changed vectorization, selection frequency, and snapshot semantics.

Report: `projects/genetic-algorithms/report.md`

### when-does-vectorized-numpy-actually-lose-to-plai — When does vectorized NumPy actually lose to plain Python loops? Characterize the array-size crossover point for common elementwise operations on Apple Silicon, and test whether the crossover is operation-dependent.

The hypothesis was refuted: `np.where` first showed a practical measured win at \(n=4\) (power-of-two bin \(k=2\)), with an all-true median speedup of 1.1071, while the reported crossover bin was \(k=2\) for both all-true and deterministic 50% masks. However, missing random-mask and intermediate-size results mean the claimed stable crossovers—and thus exact equality between conditions—cannot be independently verified.

Report: `projects/when-does-vectorized-numpy-actually-lose-to-plai/report.md`
