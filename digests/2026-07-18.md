### when-does-vectorized-numpy-actually-lose-to-plai — When does vectorized NumPy actually lose to plain Python loops? Characterize the array-size crossover point for common elementwise operations on Apple Silicon, and test whether the crossover is operation-dependent.

The hypothesis was refuted: `np.where` did not show an earlier stable crossover with a deterministic 50% mask than with an all-true mask. On an Apple M3 Pro, NumPy’s earliest measured practical win was at \(n=4\), with a 1.11× median speedup for the all-true mask, and the reported crossover bin was \(k=2\) for both conditions. However, missing intermediate and endpoint results prevent independently verifying the claimed stable crossovers.
