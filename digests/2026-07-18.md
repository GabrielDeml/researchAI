### genetic-algorithms — Genetic algorithms

The hypothesis was refuted: batch-16 replacement reduced median end-to-end runtime by 87.0% (0.524 to 0.0680 seconds), exceeding the 40% target, but neither method reached the global optimum in any of 30 runs, so the prespecified ≥15-percentage-point success loss was not observed. This does not establish equivalent optimization performance, because the 50,000-evaluation budget produced a completely floored success rate and the comparison also changed vectorization, selection frequency, and snapshot semantics.

Report: `projects/genetic-algorithms/report.md`

### when-does-vectorized-numpy-actually-lose-to-plai — When does vectorized NumPy actually lose to plain Python loops? Characterize the array-size crossover point for common elementwise operations on Apple Silicon, and test whether the crossover is operation-dependent.

The hypothesis was refuted: `np.where` beat the Python loop at the earliest tested practical size, \(n=4\), for both all-true and deterministic 50% masks, with a 1.107× median speedup for the all-true case. The reported crossover-bin difference was zero, providing no evidence that mask randomness moved the crossover earlier. However, \(n=3\) was not tested and missing intermediate and endpoint results prevent verification of the claimed stable crossover through the largest sizes.

Report: `projects/when-does-vectorized-numpy-actually-lose-to-plai/report.md`
